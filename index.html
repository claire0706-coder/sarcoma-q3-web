Clinical framing

The real question

Case context

64-year-old woman with a deep thigh mass

  • Located within the muscle layer
  • Approximately 9 × 7 × 6 cm
  • Progressively enlarged over 2 months
  • Biopsy was arranged after initial work-up
Question 3

Is biopsy appropriate?

  1. If the local orthopedic surgeon has limited sarcoma experience?
  2. If the biopsy plan was discussed with a sarcoma team beforehand?

The issue is not only who performs biopsy, but whether biopsy is part of a planned sarcoma pathway.

這張把問題重新定義。重點不是區域醫院能不能做，而是這個 biopsy 有沒有被放在 sarcoma 的診斷與治療路徑中規劃。
Suspicion threshold

Why should this mass be treated as suspicious?

Adult new soft tissue mass
Deep intramuscular
>5 cm 9 × 7 × 6 cm
Growing over 2 months

These features meet classic warning signs for possible soft tissue sarcoma.

References: NICE NG12; López-Pousa et al., SEOM Clinical Guideline of soft-tissue sarcoma, 2016.

NICE 提到 adult unexplained lump 且 increasing in size 應快速評估 soft tissue sarcoma。SEOM guideline 也提到大於 5 公分、持續變大、深於 fascia 的腫塊要懷疑 sarcoma。
Diagnostic work-up

Blood tests are useful, but not reassuring enough

Ca / P / ALP

No obvious clue for bone involvement or bone metabolism disorder.

LDH

No strong signal of high tumor burden or rapid cellular turnover.

CRP / ESR

Less supportive of infection or marked inflammatory disease.

Key point: normal laboratory data may help exclude mimics, but cannot rule out soft tissue sarcoma.
抽血正常可以讓感染、明顯發炎、骨代謝異常或高腫瘤負荷的可能性下降，但 soft tissue sarcoma 很可能抽血完全正常。
Disease map

Staging work-up defines the disease extent

Finding in this case

No abnormal imaging findings at other sites.

No evident distant disease at this stage.

Clinical meaning

  • Treatment can be planned with curative intent
  • Local control becomes a key goal
  • If metastatic disease existed, treatment sequencing would change

Staging does not name the tumor; it maps the disease and frames the treatment goal.

Reference: López-Pousa et al., SEOM Clinical Guideline of soft-tissue sarcoma, 2016.

Staging 不是要命名腫瘤，而是看疾病範圍。教案中其他部位無異常影像，代表目前沒有明顯遠端轉移，可朝局部控制與治癒性切除規劃。
Local roadmap

MRI before biopsy

Imaging description

  • Left proximal lateral thigh mass
  • Homogeneous soft tissue tumor
  • Marked contrast retention after enhancement

What MRI should clarify

  • Size, depth, and compartment
  • Relationship to bone and neurovascular structures
  • Viable area for tissue sampling
  • Future surgical field and biopsy route
Guideline principle: MRI should be performed before biopsy in suspected soft tissue sarcoma.

Reference: López-Pousa et al., SEOM Clinical Guideline of soft-tissue sarcoma, 2016.

Contrast retention 代表顯影劑在腫瘤內停留，常提示腫瘤具有實質性血流供應或較豐富的間質成分；在 myxoid tumor 中，也可能和黏液樣基質造成的延遲顯影有關。
Biopsy planning

Biopsy is not “just taking a piece”

In suspected sarcoma, the biopsy tract may become part of the future surgical field.

Wrong route

Contaminates unnecessary compartments.

Wrong target

Samples necrotic or non-representative tissue.

Harder surgery

Larger resection and functional cost.

Reference: López-Pousa et al., SEOM Clinical Guideline of soft-tissue sarcoma, 2016.

Sarcoma 的 biopsy tract 可能被腫瘤細胞污染，所以後續 definitive surgery 通常要把這條路徑一起切掉。
Biopsy method

Why core needle biopsy?

Method How it works Strengths Limitations / cautions Typical role
FNA
Fine needle aspiration		 Uses a thin needle to aspirate cells for cytology. Minimally invasive. Lower diagnostic accuracy; higher risk of non-diagnostic or non-representative samples. Not recommended as routine biopsy for suspected soft tissue sarcoma.
CNB
Core needle biopsy		 Uses a larger needle to obtain tissue core samples, often image-guided. High accuracy; outpatient local anesthesia; lower complication risk than incisional biopsy. Accuracy may be lower in some locations or histologies; negative CNB does not always exclude malignancy. Preferred first-line technique in many suspected sarcoma work-ups.
IB
Incisional biopsy		 Open surgical incision obtains a larger tissue sample. More tissue for histology, molecular tests, tissue banking, or difficult diagnosis. More invasive; incision must be planned along future resection route with careful hemostasis. Used when percutaneous biopsy is inconclusive or larger tissue is required.
Excisional biopsy Removes the whole lesion. Can be definitive for selected small superficial lesions. Not appropriate for large, deep, suspicious masses; should follow imaging. Limited to superficial tumors <3 cm and away from critical structures.
Clinical synthesis: image-guided CNB is generally favored because it balances diagnostic accuracy, low invasiveness, lower contamination risk, cost, and route control.

References: Traina et al., 2015; Okada, 2016; Lewin et al., 2013; Birgin et al., 2020; Kiefer et al., 2022.

重點不是把腫瘤整顆先挖掉，而是用足夠、正確路徑的組織診斷來規劃後續治療。這張圖是一位 78 歲病人，左腿有大型可觸摸腫塊。MRI 顯示 15 乘 9 公分的異質性顯影腫瘤，醫療團隊使用 HistoCore core needle biopsy device 取樣。初診後一天，病理就診斷為 high-grade sarcoma；五天後免疫組織化學進一步確認為 undifferentiated pleomorphic sarcoma，使團隊能在初診後七天進入跨專科 tumor board 討論。
Route selection challenge

Which biopsy route would you choose?

Look at the MRI and choose the route that is more likely to obtain diagnostic tissue.

Teaching point: Green arrow is the better diagnostic target in this case.
  • The red-arrow posterior CNBs were chosen to avoid the peroneal nerve but were inconclusive.
  • The green-arrow ventral region showed stronger enhancement and ultimately required open biopsy.
  • Two failed CNBs delayed diagnosis by about 2.5 weeks.
  • A negative or non-diagnostic CNB does not exclude malignancy; clinical, radiologic, and pathologic correlation is essential.
這是一位 19 歲女孩的腿部腫瘤 MRI。影像顯示一顆異質性、顯影明顯的腫瘤。醫療團隊一開始選擇從腫瘤後側做兩次經皮粗針切片，也就是圖中的紅色箭頭，主要是因為腫瘤腹側和腓神經關係很近，希望避開神經。不過兩次切片都沒有得到明確病理結果。後來必須從顯影更明顯的腹側區域，也就是綠色箭頭，做 open biopsy，才診斷出 extraskeletal Ewing sarcoma。這個案例造成大約兩週半的診斷延遲，也提醒我們：陰性或無法診斷的 CNB 不能直接排除惡性，臨床、影像和病理必須一起判讀。

Question 3A

Is biopsy by an inexperienced local surgeon appropriate?

Suggested answer: B. Usually inappropriate.

The concern is separating the diagnostic act from the treatment plan. Referral or pre-biopsy discussion with a sarcoma team is preferred.

Question 3B

What if the sarcoma team was consulted first?

Suggested answer: B. Potentially acceptable.

A reasonable model is: sarcoma team plans, local team executes, with complete MRI, staging, route planning, and handoff.

這題不要走極端。當地切片不是絕對不行，但前提是由 sarcoma team 規劃，區域醫院只是依計畫執行。
Take-home message

Plan first, biopsy second

1. A deep, enlarging soft tissue mass >5 cm should be treated as suspicious for sarcoma.
2. Normal laboratory tests do not rule out sarcoma; staging and MRI define the next step.
3. The biopsy route affects definitive surgery and should be planned in advance.
4. The core answer to Question 3: plan first, biopsy second.
結尾用四句話收束，讓同學記得這題的核心不是「哪家醫院」，而是「先規劃，再切片」。
